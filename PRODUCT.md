# Nice Crochet — Product Context

> **Status: draft, now grounded in real assets.** The logo (`DESIGN/`) and ~190 real product photos (`PRODUCTS/`) replaced draft 1's invented brand. Everything still marked 🟡 is an assumption or invented placeholder — confirm with the site owner (@yukiandnice) before it becomes real copy.

## Vision

A **portfolio/showcase site** for the handmade crochet brand **Nice Crochet** ("Handmade with love" — the logo's own tagline), tied to TikTok [@yukiandnice](https://www.tiktok.com/@yukiandnice) — a place for people who find the brand through TikTok to actually browse the pieces properly (search, filter by category/colour), not a transactional store. Confirmed by the owner (2026-08-23): "อันนี้เป็นแค่ระบบแสดงผลงานเท่านั้น เหมือน web โชว์ผลงานอ่ะ" — no cart, no checkout, no prices. Anyone who wants a piece still goes through DM/TikTok, same as today; this site's job is just to make the catalog browsable.

## Target users

- People who already follow @yukiandnice on TikTok and want to see the full range of what's been made, or find a specific piece they saw in a video.
- New visitors who land on the site directly and are browsing crochet work generally.

🟡 Assumed: primarily mobile visitors — the design should hold up at narrow widths first.

## Real product catalog (confirmed from `PRODUCTS/` photos, 26 of ~190 sampled)

Four real product families:

1. **Market totes & bags** — by far the dominant line. Mesh weave, lattice weave (incl. a distinctive two-tone lattice-over-solid style), and solid granny-stitch bucket totes. Handle styles vary: short double handle, long single strap, round loop handle. This is the flagship category.
2. **Pouches & purses** — small flap-top pouches, closed with a contrasting crochet loop-and-button.
3. **Card holders & wallets** — flap-tab closure, often two-tone, frequently finished with a pom-pom or flower charm keychain.
4. **Flower charms & keychains** — a signature crochet flower motif, sold standalone as a charm and also used to finish totes and card holders.

Real colourways observed across photos: cherry red, mustard/gold, camel, tan/beige, charcoal grey, sage green, plum/wine, cream, denim (raw yarn stock only, not yet seen on a finished piece). Colour is genuinely cross-category — the same tote style ships in 6–8 colours — so the shop's "search + filter" should filter by **colour**, not treat colour as a category.

Photography style observed: a mix of outdoor lifestyle shots (park benches, playgrounds, natural light) and indoor flat-lay/studio shots (marble tabletop, white/wood surface, floral props), plus some work-in-progress shots (piece still on the hook, stitch markers visible).

🟡 Still unknown from photos alone — needs owner input: real product names, exact count of catalog categories beyond the 4 observed (190 photos were sampled, not exhaustively cataloged). Prices/stock status are no longer relevant — this is a portfolio, not a store (see Vision).

## Core problems this site solves

- No current way to browse the maker's full body of work outside of scrolling TikTok/Instagram.
- No search or filter — someone who wants "the mustard tote" or "something in charcoal" currently has no way to find that except scrolling or asking directly.
- No dedicated "about the maker" page — TikTok bio space is too short to tell the actual brand story.

## Core features (this pass)

1. **Home** — carousel hero (4 slides, one per category) + a texture/detail spotlight showing the craft up close.
2. **Shop** — full catalog with text search, category filter, **colour filter** (grounded in real colour variety). No price filter/sort — there's no price to filter by (see Not in scope).
3. **About me** — maker bio, process, TikTok link.

## Not in scope (by design, not just "not yet built")

This is a showcase, not a store — these aren't deferred features, they're deliberately excluded:
- Cart, checkout, payment, prices, stock/availability status
- Account/login, order management
- Shipping/checkout logic of any kind

Anyone who wants to buy a piece still reaches out via TikTok/DM — this site doesn't change that flow, it just makes the catalog browsable first.
